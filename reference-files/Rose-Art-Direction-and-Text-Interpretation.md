The existing world-lore text is the primary interpretation source: `docs/WORLD-LORE-AND-PARTICLE-INTEGRATION.md`, together with the later biome documents and exact paragraph citations. No separately named new text file was supplied for this pass.

## Shared visual grammar

- Build tactile, muted faux-realism: material volume, seams and handling precede small functional light signatures.
- Use the broad druid / nano / neon spectrum without treating a genre, material or culture as a rank of superiority.
- Keep natural ground continuous. The hidden local hex grid supports movement; existing optional tactical overlays remain available.
- Preserve the locked orthographic projection, 0.52 ground compression, six facings, foot anchors, palette and optional CRT presentation.
- Express vast scale through body, service detail, inhabited terrace, monolith and atmospheric horizon; source dimensions do not recalibrate the map.
- Individual characters retain their registered body, appearance, personal items, history, speech, attitude and current engagement.
- Growth, weathering, repair, construction, contact and destruction follow saved outcomes and finite inputs.
- Keep particulate measurements and engineering concepts off screen during ordinary play; preserve uncertainty in authoring sources.
- Text and current state define meaning and behavior. Images guide presentation; conflicting source depictions remain variants.

This is a coherent direction for the broad druid / nano / neon spectrum. Original source text remains intact where it uses an older genre label; current user direction governs new art. Dark surfaces must retain material separation and readable contact. Tiny windows, access points and maintained equipment make immense architecture credible.

## Implemented renderer pass

The default Text + Image Direction setting applies a bounded, deterministic RGB finish to the existing rendered structures and rocks in the four active source-backed regions. It reads the object’s existing physical material identity: wood, metal, stone, crystal, fabric or polymer. A stable per-object variation keeps repeated units from receiving a single flat color.

The finish deepens material separation and handling grain while preserving the prepared silhouette, alpha, soft shadows, foot anchor, frame timing, scale, original geometry and saturated practical-light pixels. Live weather and powered hardware remain later passes. The finish creates no emission, resources, interactions or simulation ticks. Actor bodies, skin, projects, unplaced regions, remote views, void percepts and historical art styles retain their existing rendering.

Materials → Baked Surfaces provides a reversible comparison and persists locally. The bounded cache holds at most 48 frames and failures fall back to baked surfaces. This pass does not manufacture new 3D assets or turn image concepts into rigs. The included comparison is drawn by the actual native canvas renderer from matched authored regional fixtures with the same frozen layout.

## Engine and authoring integration

Current-region optional narration receives a defensive, read-only materials/light/scale brief and the shared principles. Facts remain background rather than completed actions or current observations. Remote and void narration receives no ordinary regional context. The blueprint exports a public source-register link, principles and recipe count without character or session data.

Open Art direction and text interpretation from the map/reference links. Its register, source images, guide and matched comparison are accessible through fixed local host routes. No request filename is joined to arbitrary reference paths.

## Retained source tensions

- **framework status:** CODE, FINAL, AI EDIT, MMTX and study 70 remain conceptual variants. Order/filename establishes neither precedence, executable code, a physical law nor a change to host time or numerical kernels.
- **framework needs:** RENDER.STANCE need/access declarations are setting concepts; they issue no food, water, shelter, clearance, immortality or simulated objects.
- **framework gate:** Relational Red Gate language does not remove the documented/game access boundary or open a route.
- **character identity:** Beck is a source persona with an authored transition history, not a player identity. A Nailyn engram depiction does not establish the missing person as present, alive or recruitable.
- **spectrum labels:** Poster genre names and numeric tiers remain original text. Rose uses the requested druid / nano / neon spectrum; material tiers are not rules of skill, moral, cultural or gameplay superiority.
- **map authority:** The original interpreted drawings retain navigation authority: ENACT upper left, Red Gate / Scarlet Plateau toward lower right. Poster compositions add no coordinates or destinations.
- **khi condition:** IMAGE 18 suggests a free tent-maintaining Khi; the AETHERGALE DOCX and study 19 retain Khi in a prism. Do not spawn an available Khi from the inconsistent image.
- **scale claims:** Heights, 1.5x comparisons, kilometers, miles and Greatweb extents are inconsistent source-specific prototype claims, not a world-grid or physics calibration. Preserve vast-world framing through percept/detail hierarchy.
- **tower variants:** Rings vary from solid inhabited structures to thin lightPRKT fields. Study 27 forbids bridges while other variants show them. Keep siblings without choosing final topology, traversable rings or active portals.
- **tower hills label:** Study 32 labels ENACT integration within a tower composition; it establishes neither regional co-location nor a relocated hub.
- **mesas labels:** The Mesas panorama includes WN_REZ / Lower Burgs labels associated elsewhere with WN_TOWER. Do not transplant those districts into Mesas.
- **aquisphere tree:** Study 15 is labeled AquiSphere but borrows ArK branch/trunk/root callouts and a tree silhouette. Study 47 is spherical; neither establishes a final morphology.
- **city orientation:** City studies 21–23 describe an eastern city; 25 says far northwest and no physical proximity to the southern gate. Preserve the existing interpreted atlas position.
- **composite geography:** Composite sheets reuse regional/district labels. Juxtaposition does not establish proximity, a route, shared jurisdiction or a present object.
- **hills variants:** Studies 38–39 name six peaks; 40–45 explicitly leave them unnamed. Grave-site placement, mid districts and orbit diagrams also vary. Retain DOCX jurisdiction and do not choose a peak taxonomy automatically.
- **unlabeled studies:** Sphere studies resemble AquiSphere and monumental trees resemble the ArK, but association is unconfirmed. They remain unassigned and outside current-region guidance.
- **manifestation permissions:** A pictured utility, vendor, transfer anchor or credential creates no inventory, services, clearance, free supplies, simulated mechanism or teleport route.
- **image readouts:** Integrity percentages, verification states, cycle labels and engagement logs are concept-image pixels, not live telemetry.
- **battle scenarios:** Fractbound Covenant, Enneract Trinode, Duskvoid Syndicate and Node 7B are proposed scenario elements. Different allegiances/objectives do not become live factions, NPCs, contracts or events.
- **null report variants:** NULL_FIELD constructs stay reported/unresolved. Directions, station links, annotation positions and scale vary; images confirm neither material composition nor a route beyond Red Gate.

## Rebuild and practical limits

New individually modeled regional buildings, portraits and animation bakes remain production work. A full interactive browser and final native-size art review remain separate sign-off steps. See `docs/validation-results-v018.json` for the completed automated verification and its scope.
