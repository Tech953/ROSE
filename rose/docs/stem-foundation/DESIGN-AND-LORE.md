# Stem design and source boundaries

Stem adapts THORNS into a second game. It uses the supplied WN_TOWER, CITY_CLOUD_S, ENACT_HILLS, AETHERGALE, and NULL_FIELD.OMNI documents and the two map photographs. It does not declare a THORNS ending canonical or invent a confirmed resolution for Khi.

## An integrated field adventure

Character creation and action resolution use THORNS’s six disciplines, four attributes, proficiency concepts, bounded certifications, relationship standing, and mod registration. Survival adds sleep, injuries, ailments, treatment, and institution-specific care to the existing provisions and ecology. Version 0.3 extends all interactions through one contextual resolver and adds companion designation, cyclic engagement, local businesses, infrastructure operation, and bounded hostile-construct encounters. Contracts link character capability, travel, habitat work, social checks, standing, and care benefits.

The game loop is observation, intention, validated consequence, ecological response, and a changed observation. The engine owns costs, permissions, routes, checks, supplies, and saved outcomes. Procedural prose reads those outcomes. The optional model decorates a bounded snapshot or proposes one reviewed action; it cannot directly alter state.

Players create appearance, voice, mannerisms, history, goals, and boundaries as well as mechanical training. Local conversations acknowledge these records and preserve what was said. Co-located players’ appearances also appear in the scene. The dialogue ledger and per-contact relationships give continuity across locations and restarts, within explicit record limits.

KHO, PYRI, PAMAN DAL, and Lila are source characters used in adapted encounters. Their dialogue in this game is newly authored and is not presented as a quotation from the documents. Other custodians, their visual details, roaming travelers, local vegetation names, field station, contract tasks, numerical ecology, supplies, equipment effects, business stock/prices, hostile constructs, and rewards are new sequel material. Traveling companions receive seeded, persistent biographies and unfinished personal aims; named source characters retain documentary histories and do not receive invented childhoods or private sequel outcomes.

## Geographic interpretation

The overview supplies relative shapes, labels, western Needle placement, eastern mountains and woodland, northern water/woodland, and southern swirls. The inset supplies tower rings, connecting infrastructure, grouped settlement blocks, and an approach. Document topology supplies district and checkpoint names that cannot all be read directly from the images.

The atlas converts those observations into sectors and passability. It replaces the v0.1 quadrant layout in new worlds. Scale, exact coastlines, orientation, bridge positions, district footprints, and checkpoint coordinates remain interpretations. An uncertain northeastern label is kept as a descriptive observation rather than assigned a canonical identity; Ind.Crux is a provisional transcription.

CITY_CLOUD_S sectors represent an adapted accessible approach and limited service encounters. They do not establish a newly canonical unrestricted pilgrimage road into the source city. GRAVESITE and PEAK targets are exterior boundaries. Vertical Needle topology is flattened into route approaches for this prototype; it does not reconstruct every height or institutional interior.

Remote survey leaves the body in place. Origins, affiliations, embodiment concepts, training, reputation, and typed assertions never grant admin.OS, adminENACT, resurrection, ownership of lightPROKT< persons, or entry through RED>GATE>. CHECKPOINT.RESID’s free provisions and WN_MED’s client gate are specifically represented; their broader metaphysics and institutional economy are not fully simulated.

## Presentation

A readable terminal frame holds a serif prose face and monospace commands, measurements, coordinates, and logs. Regional accents express material and social motifs: ash/green around the Needle, metallic blue at mountain approaches, earth at civilian foothills, and jade/rose across the dustlands. Only the jade/rose relationship is explicit source color language; the remaining UI palette is a design interpretation.

The world atlas is interactive geography with current position, route, commitment marker, landmark services, and provenance. Original photographs remain available for comparison. Character fields, dialogue records, survival condition, care services, and contract conditions are exposed beside the adventure. CRT effects can be disabled and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Remaining scope

This is a bounded procedural game with a local-model adapter. It does not implement unrestricted prompt physics, complete institution interiors, full lore-authoritative model dialogue, a canonical sequel chronology, unrestricted/PvP combat, necra practice, Life String extraction, unrestricted bio-mod trees, world-save import from THORNS, or public server hosting.

Companion dialogue generates procedurally from recorded lore, relationships, duties, cycles and local conditions. It is bounded by supported topics and does not claim arbitrary conversational understanding. Optional local-model prose can elaborate supplied scenes without changing mechanical records.

Character creation is comprehensive for this ruleset, not a full implementation of every source-world capability. The atlas is an interpreted playable conversion, not a scale-accurate digitization. Real-browser layout, native Windows launch, accessibility conformance, and actual local-model inference still need human testing.
