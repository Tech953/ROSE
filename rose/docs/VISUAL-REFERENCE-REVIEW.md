## Applied art direction

The shared direction is tactile faux-realism with localized functional light across the requested druid / nano / neon spectrum. Distinct silhouettes, material roughness, readable service interfaces and small signature accents distinguish individual units. Broad neon washes would obscure the industrial, organic, institutional and crystalline material differences. The 90/10 image caption is a composition suggestion, not a measured engine quota.

Scale has nested references: a person, service opening, work crew, terrace, inhabited monolith and atmospheric horizon. Continent/planetary comparisons and infi-particulate percept language remain world framing. Inconsistent image distances and scale bars do not establish a world-grid conversion. Local collision, AP distance, interaction range and foot anchors remain authoritative.

## Engine and production handoff

`visual_references.py` returns defensive copies of compact art guidance. `biomes.context` provides it to the optional narrator only for the current ordinary region. Remote and percept views retain their exclusion. Full poster text, source-character biography, conceptual access declarations, proposed battle objectives and unassigned labels do not enter this context. Current weather, material state, lighting and character records remain authoritative.

The archive uses fixed HTTP routes for registered IDs, with no arbitrary filename lookup. Its JavaScript has no session, game action endpoint or external dependency. It loads one selected original at a time and rejects stale preview events after switching or reopening.

Production sequence:

1. Model each approved unit in the existing locked projection and sprite pipeline.
2. Separate silhouette, human-scale interface, tactile roughness, wear and functional emission.
3. Render approved directions, shadows, condition states and contact-timed clips with explicit alpha and foot anchors.
4. Inspect native-size palette/CRT presentation, occlusion, path clearance and solid-pixel picking.
5. Bind motion/destruction to authoritative saved results; concept effects are not a second simulation.

These paintings and diagrams supply no rigs, transparent individual cutouts, collision geometry or directional sprite sheets. Pending work includes approved regional morphology, new individual unit/character/portrait models, directional/condition animation bakes and native-size art sign-off. Existing prepared sprites and numerical kernels remain the playable baseline.
