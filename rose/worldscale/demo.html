Pan the camera with WASD / arrow keys across a 128×128-sector world (16,384 sectors — the shipped atlas is 37×25 = 925). Bright hexes are ACTIVE chunks, dim hexes are the PREFETCH ring; chunks beyond the ring are evicted and regenerate identically from the seed if you return. Every hex is placed with the exact projection rose/static/scene.js uses, translated by the camera, and painted in the renderer's own (r, q) tile order. Demo terrain only — no game art or content. Serve the repo root over HTTP (e.g. python3 -m http.server) so the ES modules load.
