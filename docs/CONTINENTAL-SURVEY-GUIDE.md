## Scale and continuity

Africa is retained as the closest approximate size comparison. The environment is perceived against the square coverage of a planet, through an infi-particulate meta-expansion percept. Monument sizes defy comprehension. The survey contains no metric transform, GPS projection, exact area or distance conversion. Symbol size cannot be read as monument height or footprint.

The author's `void_space inhibit` phrasing describes perceptual limits of the reference. Later commentary explicitly makes `void_null` tangible and traversable. Blank cartography therefore means unmapped information, rather than a new physical prohibition. Rose provides a prepared branching `void_null` text expedition separately from baseline atlas routes; the larger drawing does not grant extra permissions or unlimited runtime storage.

The existing travel atlas remains a finite 37 × 25 survey with named destinations and continuous selection of accessible land points. The larger map is a converted source survey layered into that interface. It is not silently stretched over the prototype grid. Only the two explicit approach links connect the maps, and selecting one prepares a destination without moving the player, revealing a place or spending a turn.

Mezzes and the southern expansion described in the commentary remain recorded lore awaiting their southern map. No southern metropolis geometry is fabricated in this release.

## Source and extension

`reference/maps/continental-reference.jpg` retains the supplied photograph. `static/continental-survey.json` stores nineteen normalized feature geometries, confidence, descriptions, scale language and approach links. `static/survey.js` draws the converted view and feature highlighting. The exported `Rose-continental-survey.png` is actual native-canvas output from these records.

New canonical labels and routes can be assigned to traced features without repainting the source image. An eventual continent-to-world projection needs explicit landmarks, passability and a chosen scale model; it should not infer exact distances from this symbolic photograph.
